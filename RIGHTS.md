# Rights and attribution scope

This initial research release does not declare a blanket repository-wide reuse license for the newly authored manuscript, numerical projections, diagrams or verification utilities. They are published for research inspection and the documented statistical-verification workflow. Public visibility is not a relicensing of the excluded private material or the cited third-party work. Further reuse should respect the relevant rights holders and permissions.

The three Abjad patches contain narrow context from upstream MIT-licensed implementation code. The exact upstream copyright, permission and disclaimer notice is retained in [LICENSES/abjad-ext-nauert.txt](LICENSES/abjad-ext-nauert.txt). That MIT notice applies to its upstream material; this document does not narrow or replace those permissions or claim upstream authorship of agent-generated changes.

The statistical records are selected observations and source commitments, not a redistribution of benchmark tasks, tests, private scoring code or model weights. References and package version names are attribution and provenance, not a redistribution or relicensing of those packages and papers. Optional publishing tools are dependencies installed separately; their source, binaries and standalone font files are not vendored here. Subsets of permissively licensed DejaVu fonts are embedded in the paper PDF, accompanied by [their upstream notice](LICENSES/dejavu-fonts.txt).

See [the itemized third-party review](docs/third_party_licenses.md) and [the explicit reproduction boundaries](docs/reproduction_boundaries.md).
